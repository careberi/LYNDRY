Help

Questions

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay {{PRICE_PER_LB}} a pound when you use us and nothing when you don't. We may offer a membership later for people who send laundry every week, but you will never be required to join one.

How do I know what I'll be charged?

We weigh your laundry after collecting it and multiply by {{PRICE_PER_LB}}. A typical {{BAG_WEIGHT}} bag works out around {{ESTIMATE_RANGE}}. You get the exact figure by text before the charge lands.

How do I pay?

Before your first pickup we text you a secure link to add a card. It's handled by Stripe, our payment processor — the card number never touches this website and we never see it.

When exactly am I charged?

After we weigh your laundry, and never before. You get a text with the weight and the amount every single time. Nothing is recurring — you're only charged for an order you asked for.

Do you charge for pickup or delivery?

No. Both are included in the {{PRICE_PER_LB}} a pound.

What if something needs special handling?

Mention it when you book and we'll take care of it. If it's something we can't do, we'll tell you before we collect — not after.
