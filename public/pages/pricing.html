Questions about the price

Is there a subscription?
No. You pay {{PRICE_PER_LB}} a pound when you use us, and nothing when you don't. We may offer a membership later for people who send laundry every week, but you will never be required to join one.
How do you know what I'll be charged?
We weigh your laundry after collecting it and multiply by {{PRICE_PER_LB}}. Because the weight isn't known until then, anything we say beforehand is an estimate — a typical bag of {{BAG_WEIGHT}} works out around {{ESTIMATE_RANGE}}. You'll always be told the exact figure before paying anything.
How do I pay?
We'll arrange payment with you directly for now. There's no card required to sign up and nothing stored on this website.
What isn't included?
We currently offer wash, dry and fold only — no dry cleaning, pressing or alterations. If something in your bag needs special handling, mention it when you book and we'll take care of it.
Do you charge for pickup or delivery?
No. Both are included in the {{PRICE_PER_LB}} a pound.
