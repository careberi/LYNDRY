Help

Questions

Is there a subscription?

Yes, and it is a rate rather than a membership. A subscription is cheaper per pound than a one-time pickup, with pickups {{SUBSCRIPTION_FREQUENCIES}}. Both rates are worked out from your address, so put it in the box at the top of this page to see yours. There is nothing to join, no joining fee and no minimum number of pickups, you still pay only for the laundry you send, and you can change how often we come or cancel at any time. Cancelling never changes the price of a pickup you have already had.

How do I know what I'll be charged?

We weigh your laundry after picking it up and multiply by your rate, which the box at the top of this page will tell you for your address. A {{MINIMUM}} minimum per pickup applies either way. You get the exact figure by text as the charge goes through.

How do I pay?

Before your first pickup we text you a secure link to add a card. It's handled by Stripe, our payment processor. The card number never touches this website and we never see it.

When exactly am I charged?

Once per pickup, after we weigh it, and you are told as it happens. Nothing is taken when you book. The text with the weight and the total arrives at the moment your card is charged. You are only ever charged for a pickup, never for having an account and never on a subscription cycle: a subscription books pickups and each one is charged after it is weighed, exactly like a one-time pickup. We text you the evening before every pickup, so nothing is ever charged without warning.

What if my laundry weighs very little?

The {{MINIMUM}} minimum per pickup is the price, and it is the same {{MINIMUM}} on either rate. A small load still gets a full pickup, a full wash and fold, and a full delivery, which is what the minimum covers. Cancel before we pick up and there is nothing to pay.

Do you charge for pickup or delivery?

No. Both are included in the per-pound rate, whichever one you are on.

What if something needs special handling?

Mention it when you book and we'll take care of it. If it's something we can't do, we'll tell you before we pick up, not after.
