Questions about the price

Is there a subscription?
No. You pay {{PRICE}} per bag when you use us, and nothing when you don't. We may offer a membership later for people who send laundry every week, but you will never be required to join one.
What counts as one bag?
Roughly {{BAG_WEIGHT}} — a standard LYNDRY bag, which is about what a home washing machine takes in one load. We're not going to quibble over a pound or two. If a bag is clearly overstuffed we'll count it as two and tell you before we wash anything.
How do I pay?
We'll arrange payment with you directly for now. There's no card required to sign up and nothing stored on this website.
What isn't included?
We currently offer wash, dry and fold only — no dry cleaning, pressing or alterations. If something in your bag needs special handling, mention it when you book and we'll take care of it.
Do you charge for pickup or delivery?
No. Both are included in the {{PRICE}}.
