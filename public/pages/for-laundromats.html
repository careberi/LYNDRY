One sticker off the tag onto each bag you pack, and tap its number on the page. Then tap this order is done. That is what tells our driver to come, and we will not turn up before you have said it.

The bag tag

One tag in. However many bags out. Still one order.

You repack into your own bags, and you should - one of ours can become three of yours. The three peelable stickers are how those stay attached to the order without anybody writing anything down.

What the stickers are for

You take in L4XK92 and pack it into three of your own bags. Put -1 on the first, -2 on the second, -3 on the third, and tap those three numbers on the page.

Our driver now knows to expect three bags for that order, and each one gets scanned at the customer's door. Nobody had to tell us in advance how many it would become.

It fits around your own system

The id is six characters on purpose - short enough to say out loud across a counter. Write it on your own ticket, put it in your own book, file it however you already file drop-off work. We never need to see any of that. We only need the sticker on the bag.

The one rule

One customer's laundry never goes in with another's, whatever the weights look like. That is the one we will not bend on.

Questions

The ones we always get asked.

What do I get paid, and how?

We start by paying your retail rate - the same price a walk-in customer pays you. No haggling before you have any idea whether the work suits you.

Once we are established and bringing you steady volume, we come back and have a conversation about wholesale pricing. Not before, and not as a condition of starting.

You invoice us on the weight you entered. You never take payment from the customer and never handle their card.

How much work will I actually get?

We will not promise you a volume, and you should be wary of anyone who does. {{NAME}} is a young service in {{SERVICE_AREA}} and the honest answer is that it depends on how many customers we have in your part of it.

What we can tell you is how it is decided: bags go to whichever partner is nearest and open, so a laundromat close to where our customers live sees more work than one at the edge.

Do I have to install an app or learn software?

No. There is no app, no account, no login and no password. Your staff point the phone camera they already have at a code on a bag, and a web page opens.

That page has two things on it your staff can do: type a weight, and tap to say the order is finished.

What happens if I am full, or closed?

Nothing arrives. We hold your opening hours and a daily capacity in pounds, and the routing skips a partner who is shut or already at their limit rather than turning up and hoping.

Tell us the hours and the capacity you actually want and we will work to them. You are never sent a bag you have no room for.

Am I locked into anything?

No exclusivity and no commitment on volume. You keep your own walk-in trade and any other arrangements you have, and this is drop-off work that fits around them.

Who deals with the customer if something goes wrong?

We do. The customer's whole relationship is with {{NAME}} - they book with us, they are billed by us, and they raise anything with us.

If a problem is genuinely a wash problem we will come and talk to you about it, but your staff never take that call at the counter.

Do you put your name on my shop?

No. No signage, no stickers on your window, nothing on your counter. Your shop stays your shop.

What is the turnaround you are promising them?

Back at the customer's door the {{TURNAROUND}}. That is what the countdown on your screen is showing you, so you can always see how much time a load actually has rather than guessing.

If your turnaround is longer than that we will know before we send you anything, and it simply means some loads go elsewhere.

What sort of laundry is it?

Household wash, dry and fold from homes and apartments. No dry cleaning, no pressing, no alterations, and no commercial linen contracts.
