How it works

Two ways to hand off your laundry. Both of them start and end in a text message.

Option one

Leave it out

Put the bag by your door before you go. Nobody needs to be home, awake or anywhere nearby.

  1. 1 Text us the day you want it collected.
  2. 2 Leave the bag outside. We text you when it's collected.
  3. 3 It comes back to the same spot, folded, with a photo.
Option two

Hand it over

If you'd rather pass the bag to a person, say so when you book and the driver will knock.

  1. 1 Tell us you'd like to hand it over — we'll agree a time.
  2. 2 The driver knocks and takes the bag from you.
  3. 3 You choose the same again on delivery, or a doorstep drop.

You never need to be home

This is the part that matters, and every other step is designed around it. You don't wait in for a window. You don't rearrange a meeting. You put a bag down before you leave and it's back, folded, by this time tomorrow.

It works the same in a house or an apartment — put your unit number on the signup form and the driver comes to your door. If you manage a building and want this offered to your residents, talk to us.

Text us. Cancelling is free right up until your bag is collected. Once it's with us it's already being processed, so it can't be cancelled after that.
