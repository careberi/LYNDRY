How it works

The point of LYNDRY is that your laundry gets done without it costing you a slot in your day. Here is the whole process, start to finish.

  1. 01

    You sign up, once

    Two minutes on the signup page. Your address, your phone number, and how you like your laundry done — water temperature, detergent, fabric softener, and anything that needs special care.

    This is the only time we ask. Everything after this is one message.

  2. 02

    You text us when you need a pickup

    Something like “laundry tomorrow”. That's it — no app, no login, no form. We confirm by text.

    If something about the request isn't clear, we'll ask you one short question. Never a list of them.

  3. 03

    You leave your bag out — or don't

    Your choice, and you can change it per pickup:

    • Leave it by the door. You don't need to be home, or awake, or anywhere nearby.
    • Hand it to the driver. If you'd rather pass it over in person, say so and we'll knock.
  4. 04

    We collect and wash it

    You get a text when your bag has been collected, so you know it's with us. It's then washed exactly the way you set up at signup — nobody has to ask you anything.

  5. 05

    It comes back to your door

    Washed, dried and folded, within {{TURNAROUND}}. We text you when it's on the way and again when it's delivered — with a photo, so you know it arrived and where it was left.

You never need to be home

This is the part that matters. Every other step is designed around it.

You don't wait in for a window. You don't rearrange a meeting. You put a bag down before you leave and it's back, folded, by this time tomorrow. The only thing you actively do is send one text.

For apartment buildings we go further: LYNDRY smart lockers let residents drop laundry at any hour, and our driver collects on their own route. Your schedule and ours stop having to match at all. If you manage a building, talk to us.

Practical questions

What if I need to cancel?
Text us. Cancelling is free right up until your bag is collected. Once it's with us, it's already being processed and can't be cancelled.
