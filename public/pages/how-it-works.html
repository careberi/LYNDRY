How it works

Two ways to hand it over.

Both of them start and end in a text message.

{{ICON_PACKAGE}} Option one

Leave it out

Put the bag by your door before you go. Nobody needs to be home, awake or anywhere nearby.

  1. Text us the day you want it collected.
  2. Leave the bag outside. We text you when it's collected.
  3. It comes back to the same spot, folded, with a photo.
{{ICON_TRUCK}} Option two

Hand it over

If you'd rather pass the bag to a person, say so when you book and the driver will knock.

  1. Tell us you'd like to hand it over — we'll agree a time.
  2. The driver knocks and takes the bag from you.
  3. Choose the same again on delivery, or a doorstep drop.

The point

You never need to be home.

This is the part that matters, and every other step is designed around it. You don't wait in for a window. You don't rearrange a meeting. You put a bag down before you leave and it's back, folded, by this time tomorrow.

It works the same in a house or an apartment — put your unit number on the signup form and the driver comes to your door. If you manage a building and want this offered to your residents, talk to us.

Help

Questions

What if I need to cancel?

Text us. Cancelling is free right up until your bag is collected. Once it's with us it's already being processed, so it can't be cancelled after that.

How much fits in a bag?

A typical bag holds {{BAG_WEIGHT}} — about one full washing machine, which comes to roughly {{ESTIMATE_RANGE}} at {{PRICE_PER_LB}} a pound. We take up to {{MAX_ORDER}} in one pickup. See pricing.

Can I set detergent or drying preferences?

Yes, once. You set them at signup, and you can change them any time by text. We keep them on file so you only say it once.

Do I need my own bag?

Any bag works. Use whatever you have — a laundry sack, a duffel, a sturdy tote.

How do I pay?

Before your first pickup we text you a secure link to add a card. It's handled by Stripe, our payment processor — the card number never touches this website. After that it's charged after each wash, and you get the weight and the total before the charge lands.

Do you serve my area?

We cover {{SERVICE_AREA}}. Sign up even if you're just outside it and we'll tell you when we reach you.
