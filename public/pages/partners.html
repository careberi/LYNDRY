You have the machines.

Most laundromats have hours in the day when half the floor is doing nothing. We bring wash-and-fold work to fill them. You wash, we handle the customer, the scheduling, the driving and the billing.

  • No storefront changes and no counter staff needed
  • Bags arrive tagged and weighed, with the wash instructions attached
  • We never put your name in front of the customer unless you want us to

Rates and volume are worked out per site — tell us what capacity you have spare and we'll talk numbers.
