CONFIRMED OPERATIONAL PERCENTAGE / DYNAMIC MINIMUM - 2026-09-27

Dynamic-minimum formula remains a proposal. Recommended invariant if Neil
wants target protection: customer revenue after partner wash cost, both courier
trips, and configured processing must meet the category contribution target
at all supported order weights. Verify any partner minimums and other costs.
For a quoted rate r, operational fee f, wholesale rate c, round-trip cost d,
processing percentage s, fixed processing b, target whole-order margin m:
Let T(w) = max(M, r*w + f). Under linear wholesale cost c*w, a sufficient
minimum at the crossover is M >= (d+b-c*f/r)/(1-s-m-c/r), assuming the
denominator is positive. Also validate zero-weight and permitted weight range,
rounding, provider minimums, and cases with a nonpositive denominator.
Example only: c=1, d=17.98, s=.029, b=.30, m=.10, r=1.65, f=4.50 gives
approximately 58.71 total minimum before small rounding adjustments, NOT 30.
The per-pound rate was itself calibrated to a 33-lb reference at the target,
so a margin-protecting floor lands near the reference order's full price.
Alternative dynamic floors based only on a small reference load can recover
that small load's costs but do not guarantee the same margin at every weight.
No dynamic-minimum formula approved and no application code changed.
